DevOps bridges the gap between development and operations, fostering seamless collaboration and improving efficiency. It plays a vital role in building systems that are scalable, adaptable, and reliable.

Continuous Integration (CI) and Continuous Deployment (CD) are key components of the software development lifecycle. Their goal is to streamline delivery while maintaining quality. By automating testing and deployment, CI/CD minimizes human error, accelerates development, and ensures a more consistent and reliable release cycle.
