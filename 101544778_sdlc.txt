DevOps Integrate software development and IT operations to accelerate the software development life cycle 
while imporving product quality and reliability. 
One of the main benefits is its speed of delivery to code. 
By automating build, test, and release processes, DevOps eliminates manual bottlenecks. 
Teams would be able to push code to production in minutes rather than months, allowing organizations to respond 
rapidly to market demands and customer feedback.
